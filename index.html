AVATAR VISUALISER
developed by sub-surface
Real-time 3D audio visualiser in the style of Joy Division's Unknown Pleasures. Scanlines are displaced by live FFT data.
getting started: burden.wav is pre-loaded. Click preview top-right to start, then explore the parameter menu bottom-right to sculpt the visual response.
control map
1—4   switch vis modes
space   toggle export/live
f   fullscreen   |   p   params
esc   exit clean mode
workflow & integration
export — drag & drop audio or load via menu. Renders MP4 with baked metadata overlays.
live — click to grant mic access. Optimized for streaming via ?clean or ?obs URL parameter.
